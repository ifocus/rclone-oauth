Privacy policy

For the OAuth client named rclone-oauth. Last updated 20 November 2026.

Who this covers

This OAuth client is used by a single individual to let their own installation of rclone, an open-source file-synchronisation tool, reach their own Google Drive. It has no other users.

What data is accessed

When authorised, the client can read and write files in the Google Drive account of the person who authorised it, and only that account. It is used to synchronise that person's own files between their own computers and their own Google Drive.

What data is collected or stored

None. There is no server, no database, no analytics and no logging operated by this client. File contents are transferred directly between the owner's computers and Google's servers. The access credentials are stored only in the rclone configuration file on the owner's own machines.

What data is shared

None. No data is sold, shared, transferred to any third party, or used for advertising, profiling or training. No one other than the account owner has access through this client.

Retention and deletion

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing retained to delete. The owner can revoke this client's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page, which immediately ends its access.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address.
